Oneweek a planner
for a world in flux

What it is

One week on a screen — everything important in view. Plan for the week you're living in now, without noise from far-off deadlines and endless lists.

Features

  • Custom themes — your own text color, background, and font for the mood or task at hand.
  • Time-anchored tasks — pin something to a moment during the week so it doesn't get lost in the flow.
  • Shared task inbox — tasks without a specific day that you can drag into any date when the time comes.
  • Color coding — highlight what matters most so it stands out at a glance.

Free, period

  • No ads — ever.
  • Every feature that exists today and everything we add later stays free for everyone.
  • No spam or newsletters — your email is only for signing in and saving your tasks.
